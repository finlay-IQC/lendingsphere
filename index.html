Good to know before you apply

Unsecured means no property security. There's no charge over your property or business assets. However, directors are typically asked to provide a personal guarantee.

Speed is the trade-off. Unsecured facilities decide in days rather than weeks, which is why they carry higher rates than secured lending. For the right situation, that speed is worth far more than the rate difference.

Terms typically run 1–5 years. Your qualification call is where we match the facility length to your actual situation.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does 'unsecured' actually mean?

No charge is taken over your property or business assets. However, directors are typically required to provide a personal guarantee. If you'd prefer a secured facility, we can discuss that on your qualification call.

How fast can funds land?

Once your case is with a lender and they have what they need, decisions and drawdown are typically measured in days rather than weeks. Exact timing depends on your situation and the lender.

Why is there an application fee?

The fee covers a full assessment of your case, matching with the right lender, and end-to-end management of the process. It filters for business owners who are serious, and it's credited against your success fee when your funding completes.

What if I don't meet the criteria?

We'll tell you on the qualification call rather than string you along. In some cases there may be alternative routes we can point you towards.

Do you lend the money yourselves?

No. Lending Sphere is a commercial finance broker. We match your case with lenders on our panel and broker network, and funding is provided by those lenders, subject to their approval.
